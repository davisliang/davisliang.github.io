I lead the Machine Learning Team at [Abridge AI](https://www.abridge.com/) and apply my research in multilinguality, automatic speech recognition (ASR), and large language models to build state-of-the-art clinical intelligence for doctors and patients. Previously, I was a Senior Research Scientist at [Meta AI](https://ai.facebook.com/) working on large-scale pretraining of multilingual language models.

Prior to Meta, I focused on question answering, information retrieval, machine translation, and speech recognition as an Applied Scientist at Amazon (AWS) AI. I was also a Software Engineer at Yahoo and obtained my MS degree in Computer Science from UC San Diego, where I was advised by [Prof. Gary Cottrell](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~gary/).
